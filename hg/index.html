Getting Ahead: AP Human Geography

Explore how people, places, cultures, cities, and economies shape the world. Get ready to earn that 5!

📝 AP Exam Breakdown

The AP Human Geography exam is 2 hours and 15 minutes long and has two sections.

Section Question Type # of Questions Time % of Score
Section I Multiple Choice 60 questions 1 hour 50%
Section II Free Response 3 questions 1 hour 15 minutes 50%

📘 Core Units You'll Learn

🗺️ Unit 1: Thinking Geographically

Maps, spatial patterns, scale, regions, and geographic tools.

Exam Weight: 8–10%
👥 Unit 2: Population and Migration

Population patterns, density, migration, and demographic change.

Exam Weight: 12–17%
🗣️ Unit 3: Cultural Patterns and Processes

Culture, language, religion, diffusion, and identity.

Exam Weight: 12–17%
🏛️ Unit 4: Political Patterns and Processes

States, nations, borders, boundaries, and political power.

Exam Weight: 12–17%
🌾 Unit 5: Agriculture and Rural Land Use

Farming, food production, rural settlement, and land-use patterns.

Exam Weight: 12–17%
🏙️ Unit 6: Cities and Urban Land Use

Urbanization, city models, suburbs, planning, and inequality.

Exam Weight: 12–17%
🏭 Unit 7: Industrial and Economic Development

Industrialization, development, globalization, trade, and services.

Exam Weight: 12–17%
💡 Study Tip

Practice using geographic terms with real-world examples. Focus on maps, population data, cultural diffusion, political boundaries, agriculture, urban models, and economic development patterns.
